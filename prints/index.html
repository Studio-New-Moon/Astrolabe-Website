Something from Follow My Astrolabe, off the screen and onto a wall — printed to order on real paper and shipped to you flat.

The object

Prints are produced on 170 gsm coated silk stock, in landscape, at the proportions the image was composed in — so nothing is cropped to fit a frame it was never meant for. Each one is made when it's ordered rather than pulled from a shelf, by a print partner who produces it and ships it directly to you.

Three sizes, from one that suits a shelf to one that takes a wall:

  • 12 × 16 in — 300 × 400 mm
  • 18 × 24 in — 450 × 600 mm
  • 24 × 32 in — 600 × 800 mm

Prices are shown at checkout.

Ordering

Prints are ordered from inside Follow My Astrolabe, which is where the chart they come from already lives. The app isn't on the App Store yet — when it arrives, this is where the prints live.

Buying directly from this page is something we're building. It isn't ready, and we'd rather say so than leave a button here that doesn't work.
