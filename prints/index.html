Something from Follow My Astrolabe, off the screen and onto a wall — printed to order on real paper and shipped to you flat.

The Object

Prints are produced in landscape, at the proportions the image was composed in — so nothing is cropped to fit a frame it was never meant for. Each one is made when it's ordered rather than pulled from a shelf, by a print partner who produces it and ships it directly to you.

Three sizes, from one that suits a shelf to one that takes a wall:

  • 12 × 16 in — 300 × 400 mm
  • 18 × 24 in — 450 × 600 mm
  • 24 × 32 in — 600 × 800 mm

And five papers: a semi-gloss and a matte, each in a standard and a heavier weight, and a 250 gsm archival matte on off-white stock made to last. The price below changes with the paper you choose.

Or on something other than paper: framed, on canvas, metal, or acrylic, or on a T-shirt, sweatshirt, tote bag, or mug. On the last four you can have the painting with its cream border and title, or the painting alone.
