How it works on a browser

There's no account and no password. When you subscribe we email you a link; opening it unlocks the readings in that browser. Open the same link on your laptop and that browser is unlocked too. Reading somewhere new, or cleared your browser? Ask for another link from your account page.

What the site keeps is your email address and whether the subscription is current — nothing about your chart. The privacy policy spells it out.
