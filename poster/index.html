How It Works

  1. Choose whose chart it is and the skin tone for its people, then pay $9.99.
  2. We make the artwork from that chart. A person checks and refines every one before it reaches you, and it's ready within three days.
  3. We email you a link. Look at it, then approve it or ask for a change.
  4. Once you've approved it, download it, and if you like, order a print of it on any of our papers and sizes.
