Support

Last updated: 2026-08-25

Follow My Astrolabe is made by Studio New Moon, a very small studio. There is no support queue and no bot — mail goes to a person, and we answer as quickly as we can.

Email us feedback@followmyastrolabe.com

This is the same address the envelope button inside the app writes to, so you can reach us either way. Telling us your device, your iOS version, and what you were doing when something went wrong makes a real difference in how fast we can fix it.

Subscriptions

What's free, and what isn't

A real computed birth chart, your daily horoscope, the full gallery of signs, the Sky view, one Tarot draw a day, and ordering prints are all free, with no account and no sign-up. Astrolabe Premium adds the deeper per-placement readings, weekly, monthly and yearly horoscopes, and further Tarot readings.

Managing or cancelling

Premium is an auto-renewing subscription billed through your Apple Account, so Apple handles the billing side of it. To change tiers or cancel, open Settings on your device, tap your name at the top, then Subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next renewal; your access continues to the end of the period you already paid for.

Restoring a purchase

If you've subscribed before — on a new phone, or after reinstalling — open Settings inside the app and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple Account you originally subscribed with; that account is what the subscription belongs to.

Refunds

Apple processes all App Store purchases, and refunds are requested from Apple rather than from us, at reportaproblem.apple.com. If something in the app is the reason you're asking, we'd like to know — write to us as well, and we'll try to put it right.

Print Orders

Chart to Canvas turns your own chart into a physical print, paid for through Stripe and produced and shipped by our print partner. If an order hasn't arrived, arrived damaged, or looks wrong, email us with the address you ordered to and roughly when you placed it, and we'll chase it down.

The Sky View

Sky uses your device's compass, motion sensors, and location to work out which part of the sky you're holding the phone up to. If you'd rather not grant those permissions, the app falls back to a slider you can move by hand — nothing is locked behind saying yes. Your location is used on the device to orient the sky and is never sent to us.

Your Data

Your birth details, your chart, and any photo you add stay on your device. There are no accounts, so there's nothing for us to look up on your behalf — and nothing for us to lose. Deleting the app removes what it stored. The full details are in the Privacy Policy.

For questions specifically about privacy, the policy lists morgan@followmyastrolabe.com; either address reaches us.

Requirements

Follow My Astrolabe runs on iPhone and iPad with iOS 18 or later. An Android version isn't available yet.
