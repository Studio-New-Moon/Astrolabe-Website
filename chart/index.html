Follow My Astrolabe

Cast a Chart.

The same instrument the app draws — a real astrolabe, with the houses cut into the fixed limb, the sky over your own latitude on the plate beneath, and the zodiac turning on the rete above.

Nothing you type here leaves your device. The chart is worked out in this page, by your own browser. There is no server to send it to.

It opens on Alex Rivera — an invented person, 16 March 1990, 2:15 PM, New York — so the page shows what it does before you type anything. The defaults here are fictional on purpose: a real birth time, to the minute, with a place, identifies someone.

Keep it on your Home Screen: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. It opens like an app, and it works with no signal.
