Follow My Astrolabe

Cast a Chart.

A real astrolabe, with the houses cut into the fixed limb, the sky over your own latitude on the plate beneath, and the zodiac turning on the rete above.

Nothing you type here leaves your device. The chart is worked out right here in your own browser, and there's no server to send it to.

The chart below is for Alex Rivera, an invented person born 16 March 1990 at 2:15 PM in New York. Put in your own birth details to cast yours.

Keep it on your Home Screen: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. It opens like an app, and it works with no signal.
